Better Imagine previews, dragging, and copy handoff.

- **Richer Copy handoff**: **Copy** now captures a screenshot of the implemented preview change in
  place on the window (saved to your screenshots folder and placed on the clipboard), reads the
  current open browser tab and URL when targeting a browser, and includes implementation
  instructions guiding your project's AI to follow its design system and guardrails.
- **Movable Imagine frame**: drag the Imagine frame anywhere on screen (before or after previewing)
  to compare side by side or move it out of the way; double-click to snap it back.
- **Immersive preview mode**: clicking **✕** on the preview prompt bar (or pressing **Esc**) now
  hides the controls and dims the Imagine border instead of dismissing the preview. Hover over the
  preview to reveal **✦ Imagine** and reopen the prompt input, or click the trash button to remove
  the preview completely.

Requires macOS 14 or later on Apple Silicon. This build is signed with Desk Draw's own certificate and not notarized; Screen Recording permission carries over from 0.6.1 and later.
