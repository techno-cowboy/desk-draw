Everything you draw can now be changed after you draw it.

- Pick the new **Select** tool (**V**) and click a mark to select it.
- Drag a mark to move it, or nudge it with the arrow keys (Shift moves 10 points).
- Drag a handle to resize it. Shift keeps the proportions, or snaps lines and arrows to 45° steps.
  Dragging a corner of text scales its font.
- Pick a color, size or fill to restyle the selected mark. Selecting a mark shows its current style
  in the toolbar.
- Press **⌫** to delete the selected mark, or **Esc** to deselect it.
- Picking a color or size while typing text now applies it to that text.

Every change can be undone.

Requires macOS 14 or later on Apple Silicon. This build is ad-hoc signed and not notarized; macOS may require **Open Anyway** on first launch and renewed Screen Recording permission after an update.
