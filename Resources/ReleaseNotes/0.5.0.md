Desk Draw now has a whiteboard.

- Click the new whiteboard button in the toolbar, or press **B**, to cover the display with a white
  board. All tools work on it, and white ink switches to black so it stays visible.
- Press **⌘S** or click **Save** to save the whole board as an image. It goes to the screenshots
  folder, is copied to the clipboard, and needs no Screen Recording permission.
- Press **B** again to go back to drawing on the screen. Your marks stay.
- A whiteboard with unsaved drawings is never discarded by accident. **Esc**, the close button,
  **⌥⇧D**, **Stop Drawing** and **Quit** first ask whether to save it, discard it or keep drawing.
- If the display layout changes while you draw, an unsaved whiteboard is saved before drawing stops.

Requires macOS 14 or later on Apple Silicon. This build is ad-hoc signed and not notarized; macOS may require **Open Anyway** on first launch and renewed Screen Recording permission after an update.
